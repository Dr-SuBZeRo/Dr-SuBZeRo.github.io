APPLICATION PRIVACY · CASHQ

CashQ privacy policy

Privacy information for CashQ, the offline-first Indian Rupee cash counting application.

Developer: SuBZeRo · Application ID: com.cashq · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Information you enter

CashQ processes denomination quantities, calculated totals, optional session titles and timestamps, saved counting sessions, coin visibility and denomination-counting preferences.

Local storage and accounts

Saved sessions are stored in a local Room database and preferences on your device. CashQ does not require registration, a phone number, email address or developer-operated account. Counting, calculations, history and preferences work offline.

The current manifest enables Android backup. Depending on Android version and device backup settings, app data may be included in Android or manufacturer backup services, which have separate privacy terms.

Network access and update checks

CashQ declares internet access for its documented manual update-check feature. When you select Check for updates, the app contacts the GitHub Releases API for the CashQ repository. This is user-initiated and does not run as background polling. GitHub may receive standard connection information, including your IP address. Cash counting does not need a network connection.

Sharing and saved images

If you choose to share a text summary, image or email-formatted report, CashQ passes it to Android's Sharesheet or the destination you select. Images you save are written to the device's Pictures/CashQ gallery location. The receiving app or service applies its own privacy policy.

Permissions, analytics and advertising

The reviewed manifest declares INTERNET and a legacy external-storage write permission limited to API 28 and below. Android FileProvider is used for temporary user-initiated file sharing. No location, contacts, microphone or camera permission is declared in the reviewed manifest. CashQ is documented as having no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs; release dependencies should be rechecked before publication.

Retention and deletion

Saved sessions remain until deleted in CashQ or app data is removed. Images saved to the gallery remain until deleted using your device's gallery or file manager. Android backups may retain separate copies if enabled.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to submit personal details to the developer through an app account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date. Release notes may also describe material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a discussion or issue through the SuBZeRo GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal, financial, business or patient information in a public issue.
