APPLICATION PRIVACY · STOCKQ

StockQ privacy policy

Privacy information for StockQ, an offline-first inventory and small-business recordkeeping application.

Developer: SuBZeRo · Application ID: com.subzero.stockq · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Business information you may store

Depending on the modules used, StockQ records may include shop details, products and stock quantities, prices, suppliers, purchase entries and invoices, customers and payment dues, jobs, batches and expiry details, quotations, employees, wages, expenses and document metadata or files. These may be confidential business or personal information.

Local storage and network access

The current Android manifest declares no INTERNET permission and describes StockQ as strictly offline-first. Core data is stored locally. StockQ does not require an online account, phone number, advertising service, analytics service or cloud backend for documented core workflows. The developer does not receive business records through a StockQ-operated server.

Backup and restore

The current manifest disables Android automatic app backup. Project documentation describes user-controlled backup and restore using Android's Storage Access Framework. When you explicitly create a backup, you choose the destination. A selected cloud or storage provider may process the file under its own privacy terms. Backup files may contain sensitive business records and should be protected.

Permissions and third-party services

The reviewed manifest does not declare INTERNET permission. Android's document picker and Storage Access Framework mediate access to files and locations you select. Project documentation specifies no advertising, analytics or telemetry SDKs and no automatic server upload; release dependencies and behaviour should be audited before distribution.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in local app storage until deleted in the app or app data is removed. Backup files exported by you are separate copies and are not automatically deleted when records are removed from StockQ.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to submit personal details to the developer through an app account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date. Release notes may also describe material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a discussion or issue through the SuBZeRo GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal, financial, business or patient information in a public issue.
