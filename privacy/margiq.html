APPLICATION PRIVACY · MARGIQ

MargiQ privacy policy

Privacy information for MargiQ, the Android pricing, tax and profit calculation utility.

Developer: SuBZeRo · Application ID: com.margiq · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Information you enter

MargiQ processes purchase and selling prices, tax rates and tax modes, units per pack, supplier discounts, free-stock offers, and product or supplier comparison values. Saved calculations and their results are stored in the app's local database on your device.

Local storage and accounts

MargiQ does not require an account, phone number, email address or cloud account for core calculator features. Calculation history and preferences are stored locally. The developer does not receive saved calculations through a MargiQ-operated backend.

The current manifest enables Android backup. Depending on Android version, device settings and backup configuration, app data may be included in platform or manufacturer backup services, which have separate privacy terms.

Network access and update checks

The app declares internet access. Its documented network feature is a user-initiated check for published releases from the official MargiQ GitHub repository. GitHub may receive standard connection information, including your IP address. Calculations and saved history do not require an internet connection.

Sharing and file access

When you choose to share a calculation summary or image, MargiQ passes the selected content to Android's sharing or storage mechanisms. You choose the destination. The receiving app may process the information under its own policy. File sharing uses Android FileProvider to grant temporary access to the selected file.

Permissions, analytics and advertising

The reviewed manifest declares INTERNET and a legacy external-storage write permission limited to Android API 28 and below. No location, contacts, microphone or camera permission is declared in that manifest. Project documentation states that MargiQ is designed without advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs; this must be rechecked against each release's final dependencies and behaviour.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain on your device until deleted in the app or the app's data is removed. Device backups, if enabled, may retain separate copies. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete backup copies.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to submit personal details to the developer through an app account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date. Release notes may also describe material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a discussion or issue through the SuBZeRo GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal, financial, business or patient information in a public issue.
