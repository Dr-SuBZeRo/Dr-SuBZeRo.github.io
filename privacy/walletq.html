APPLICATION PRIVACY · WALLETQ

WalletQ privacy policy

Privacy information for WalletQ, the local-first personal and household finance application.

Developer: SuBZeRo · Application ID: com.walletq · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Financial information you may store

Depending on the features used, WalletQ records may include account names and balances, income and expenses, transfers, currencies and exchange rates, budgets, savings information, household profiles and related notes. Financial records can be highly sensitive; protect access to your device and exported files.

Local storage and accounts

The current Android manifest does not declare INTERNET permission. WalletQ uses a local Room database and on-device preferences. It does not require a developer-operated account, phone number or cloud service for its documented core use. The developer does not receive financial records through a WalletQ backend.

The manifest enables Android backup and references Android data-extraction and full-backup rules. Depending on those rules, Android version and device settings, app data may be included in platform or manufacturer backup services, governed by separate terms.

Network and third-party services

Based on the current manifest reviewed for this policy, WalletQ does not request Android INTERNET permission. If future versions add online features or external integrations, this policy and store disclosures must be updated before release.

Export, sharing and backups

Any export, sharing or backup creates or transfers a separate copy of financial information. You choose the destination. Files saved outside WalletQ may remain after app data is deleted and may be accessible to other apps or people with access to that location.

Analytics, advertising and retention

WalletQ is intended to operate without advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Records remain in local app storage until deleted through available controls or app data is removed. Exported files and platform backup copies may persist independently.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to submit personal details to the developer through an app account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date. Release notes may also describe material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a discussion or issue through the SuBZeRo GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal, financial, business or patient information in a public issue.
