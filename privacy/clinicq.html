ClinicQ privacy policy

Pre-release privacy notice for ClinicQ, a clinic operations and patient-record management project.

Developer: SuBZeRo · Application ID: com.clinicq · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Pre-release notice: ClinicQ is documented as an early-development project. This page describes intended privacy principles, not a verified production implementation. Do not use an unreleased or unreviewed build as the sole system for patient care or medical records. This notice must be reviewed after a complete release-build audit.

Planned information handled

ClinicQ is intended to support clinic operations. Planned records may include clinic and doctor details, patient identity and contact information, appointments, visit history, queue status, follow-up reminders, prescriptions, clinical documents and basic financial records. Some of this information is sensitive personal and health information.

Intended storage and connectivity

Project documentation describes a local-first design using Room and DataStore, with no mandatory account or cloud service planned for core workflows. However, the public repository currently documents an early-development stage and does not provide a complete release manifest or verified data-flow audit. Network permissions, backup behaviour, encryption, external SDKs and actual storage boundaries cannot yet be confirmed.

Patient data and clinic responsibility

ClinicQ does not independently establish a lawful basis for processing patient information. Before using it with real patient data, the clinic must assess applicable Indian privacy and healthcare requirements, provide appropriate notices and obtain consent where required, restrict staff access, secure devices, define retention and deletion procedures, and maintain suitable backup and incident-response processes.

Backups, export and sharing

Backup and restore are part of the project roadmap, but the final implementation and destination controls are not verified. Do not assume patient records are encrypted, backed up, synchronised or recoverable in any particular way. Future export, print, share or cloud integrations must be disclosed in a release-specific policy.

Analytics, advertising and production release

Project goals specify no advertising or analytics/telemetry unless explicitly reviewed and approved. Actual release dependencies and SDK behaviour must be audited before this can be stated as a production guarantee. Before public release, this notice must identify exact data collected, permissions, storage and encryption model, backup behaviour, network endpoints, third-party SDKs, retention periods and contact route.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require users to submit personal details to the developer through an app account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, permissions, dependencies or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date. Release notes may also describe material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a discussion or issue through the SuBZeRo GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal, financial, business or patient information in a public issue.
