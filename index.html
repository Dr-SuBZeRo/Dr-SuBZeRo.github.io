Technology with principles.

Good software solves a problem without taking more from people than it gives.

Privacy by design

Collect as little as possible. No tracking, minimal permissions, and your data stays yours.

Offline first

Core features work without an internet connection wherever the product allows it.

Free to use

No advertisements, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Useful tools should stay accessible.

Open development

Public repositories, clear release notes and an open path for feedback and contributions.
